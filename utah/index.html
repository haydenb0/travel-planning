The Plan

Home for the Holidays, Utah-Style

Drive up, collect Max from school, and spend Christmas together in the snow before heading home.

From there it's paleontology and butterflies at Thanksgiving Point in Lehi and the Luminaria lights that night. 🧊 Utah traction law

When conditions call for it, UDOT can require traction devices, including on US-40 in Wasatch and Summit Counties (hotel ⇄ Park City) and I-80 in Parleys Canyon (Dec 26 drive to Tooele).

  • AWD/4WD: M+S tires on all four wheels at minimum
  • 2WD: 3-peak mountain snowflake tires, or chains or socks on the drive wheels

Weather Plan

The 9 AM pickup on Dec 23 is fixed, so any extra day goes on the front end. We have the whole week off, so we can take it.

Plan A · Clear forecast

One-day drive

Leave Simi 6 AM Tue Dec 22, sleep in Tooele, pickup at 9 AM. This is the baseline plan.

Plan B · Storm forecast

Leave Mon Dec 21

Drive ~6 hrs to St. George and stay the night. On Dec 22, drive ~4–4½ hrs to Tooele in daylight, with time to wait out a morning storm. If you arrive early, the Natural History Museum of Utah is about 45 min away (open until 5 PM).

Plan C · Caught mid-drive

Stop short

If it turns ugly on Dec 22, stop in Cedar City or Beaver and finish at dawn Dec 23. Beaver to Tooele is about 2¾ hrs, so leaving by 6 AM still makes 9 AM.

🧰 Winter car kit

  • Chains or socks that fit your tires, plus gloves to put them on
  • Ice scraper and snow brush
  • Blankets and a warm layer for everyone
  • Water, snacks, phone chargers and a power bank
  • Flashlight, jumper cables, small shovel
  • Washer fluid rated below freezing (top it off before leaving)

❄️ What to expect

  • Late-December days are short: sunrise ≈ 7:45 AM, sunset ≈ 5:05 PM in Salt Lake.
  • Heber Valley is a cold mountain valley. Plan for highs around freezing and nights well below. Luminaria is outdoors at night, so dress warm.
  • Never let the tank drop below half between Beaver and Nephi. Services are sparse there.

Know Before You Go

The Main Events

Details from each venue's website. Tap to expand.

🦕

Museum of Ancient Life

Thanksgiving Point, LehiWed Dec 23 · 10:15 AMMax's pick
▾

Mountain America Museum of Ancient Life has 60 complete dinosaur skeletons, one of the largest mounted-dinosaur displays in the world, and more than 50 hands-on exhibits. There's a working paleontology lab where staff and volunteers are excavating a 150-million-year-old Barosaurus in view of visitors. Newer exhibits include Need for Speed in Jurassic Hall (Allosaurus, Ceratosaurus, Tanycolagreus) and a Sauropods gallery where you can stand next to a sauropod for scale.

  • Lab Live: daily at 11 AM, included with admission.
  • Paleo Studio ($10): prepare a real Eocene fossil and take it home. Book early. A good fit for Max.
  • Dino Quest ($8) runs Tue/Thu/Sat at 12:30, so not on Wednesday the 23rd.
  • Geode cracking: $10 medium, $18 large.
Hours Mon–Sat 9 AM–8 PMClosed Sundays, ChristmasOnline $24 adult · $19 youth 13–17 · $17 childEntry timed, limited
🦋

Butterfly Biosphere

Thanksgiving Point, LehiWed Dec 23 · ~1:15 PMJulia's pick
▾

Utah's only butterfly conservatory and insectarium. The tropical conservatory has more than a thousand live butterflies from around the world, which makes a warm break from December. The Discovery Zone has hundreds of live insects and a 120,000-ant leafcutter colony.

  • Butterfly's First Flight ($4): help release newly emerged butterflies.
  • Daily programs: Critter Encounters, Bug Races, Entomologist Chats.
  • Timing tip from the venue: butterflies are most active at midday. Owl butterflies fly in the evening.
  • Butterflies can't leave the conservatory. Pinned specimens are sold in the gift shop.
Hours Mon–Sat 9 AM–8 PMClosed Sundays, ChristmasOnline $24 / $19 / $17
Buy the All-Access Pass. It's $39 adult, $33 youth or $29 child, and covers all seven Thanksgiving Point venues for the day. Two single-venue tickets cost more ($48 per adult online).
✨

Luminaria

Ashton Gardens, Thanksgiving PointWed Dec 23 · 5:30 PMLast night before the Christmas closure
▾

Billed as Utah's largest Christmas light display, set across the 50-acre gardens. You start at the luminary hill, where 6,500 programmable luminaries and a 120-ft tree make animated images synced to holiday music. The Fire & Ice show at the Waterfall Amphitheatre runs about 8 minutes every 20 minutes, with flames shooting over 20 ft. The Tree of Life garden has 130 bronze sculptures and is included with admission.

  • Plan at least 1.5 hours. The Tree of Life adds 30–45 min.
  • Timed entry every 15 minutes. All sales are final, and same-day changes need an exchangeable ticket.
  • Last season's hours for Dec 23 were 4–9:30 PM, with last entry 9:45. Closed Sundays, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.
  • Extras: food punch cards ($18 for 5) and private fire pits ($120 for 2 hrs, admission not included).
  • Tickets: Youth 13–17 and Child 3–12 pricing. Prices vary by date.
Address 3900 N Garden Dr, LehiDress outdoors at night: full winter layers
🛷

Soldier Hollow Snow Tubing

Wasatch Mountain State Park, MidwayThu Dec 24On sale Oct 19
▾

Long 1,200-foot lanes with a lift back to the top, at the Nordic venue of the 2002 Winter Olympics. The hill runs day and night under lights, and sessions last 2 hours. The 2026–27 season is scheduled for Dec 18, 2026 – Mar 13, 2027, depending on weather.

  • Tickets on sale Mon Oct 19, 10 AM (Mountain Time). Holiday dates use a separate "Holiday" ticket button.
  • Non-refundable, with no refunds for weather or snow. That's another reason to hold an alternative in mind.
  • Pricing changes by date and time. A recent guide put it at $25–40.
  • Kids under 3 aren't allowed, and nobody rides on a lap.
  • Colder days make for faster runs, while warm or fresh-snow days are slower.
Address 2002 Soldier Hollow Ln, MidwayInfo 435-709-3463Christmas Eve hours not posted
RiskOpening day is only about a week before Dec 24. A dry December could mean limited lanes. If tickets sell out or the hill isn't ready, see Plan B.
🏔️

Park City on Christmas Day

Historic Main StreetFri Dec 25 · afternoon–evening~25 min from hotel
▾

Park City is a ski town, so it stays open on Christmas more than most places. Walk Historic Main Street as the lights come on, look at the window displays and the Snow Globe Art Stroll (it ran through Jan 1 last season), then have an early dinner at Squatters. Plan it as an evening out, not a shopping trip. Many shops will be closed or keep short hours on the 25th.

  • Arrive around 2:45 PM to see Main Street in daylight and at dusk.
  • For a free stop on the way, the Utah Olympic Park museums are near Kimball Junction. Christmas Day hours
  • Driving home: US-40 can come under the traction law. Check UDOT before leaving.

Park City · ~25 min

Woodward Park City Tubing Tubing backup

This is the former Gorgoza Park hill. Four lanes run from the top of a 1,200-ft hill, plus three shorter mid-mountain lanes, with two carpet lifts. Tickets are limited per time slot, so book ahead. A recent guide listed 2-hour sessions at $55–89, more than Soldier Hollow.

Best fit: Dec 24 if Soldier Hollow sells out, or Dec 25 afternoon before Main Street. Holiday hours
🥇
Utah Olympic Park · Park City

Olympic Bobsled Ride For Max

A real run on the 2002 Olympic track at 60+ mph with a pro pilot. The ride is under a minute, and the visit takes about 1¼ hrs including orientation. Ages 13+ and at least 100 lb. Under-18s need a parent to sign the waiver. The park's two Olympic and ski museums are free.

$225, or $249 Dec 26–30 · Dates released Oct 26. Dec 24/25 operation
♨️
Homestead Resort · Midway

Homestead Crater

A 10,000-year-old geothermal spring inside a 55-ft limestone dome, with ~90–96°F water, 35 ft deep. You can soak, swim, snorkel or take a beginner scuba session. Soak sessions are about 40 min, and life jackets are required for swimming.

Reservations recommended · 435-657-3840 · Soaks started around $16 (older listing). Holiday hours

Midway Ice Rink

A large outdoor public rink with daily public sessions, skate rentals, a warming hut and concessions. It usually opens around Dec 1. Easy to add on Christmas Eve afternoon.

A museum built over a 200-million-year-old Early Jurassic lakeshore, with rare tracks still in the rock where they were found, fossil fish and plants, and a fossil prep lab. Max will be back at school by then, so this is a bonus for whoever else wants to stop.
